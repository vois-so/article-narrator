---
title: Why we added a listen button
description: Some readers like to listen.
pubDate: 2026-11-05
---

Some readers like to listen while they walk or cook. Thus each post on this site now has a small player at the top.

The player does not track you. It sends no request until you select **Play**, and it keeps no data in your browser.

You can use the keyboard:

- Tab moves to the next control.
- The left and right arrows move the position by 5 seconds.
- Space or Enter starts and stops the audio.

The audio is made on the computer of the writer with [Vois Studio](https://vois.so), before the site goes live.
